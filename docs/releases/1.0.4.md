StorageLens 1.0.4 fixes duplicate ownership and stale storage locations in Item Search.

### What changed

- Loadout items remain searchable but no longer increase owned item counts or
  estimated value.
- Museum items that have been borrowed and are held elsewhere no longer appear
  as stored in the Museum.
- Moving items from Ender Chest or backpack pages into the player inventory now
  removes their old storage locations immediately.
