StorageLens 1.0.6 reduces stuttering when closing chests.

### What changed

- Closing a chest no longer compresses and writes all remembered island chests
  and storage pages on the game thread.
- Changed observations are saved in the background after a short delay, with
  pending saves combined so only the latest contents are written.
- Unchanged observations skip serialization, and open containers avoid repeated
  item-stack copies when their contents have not changed.
- Pending saves finish before profile changes, cache clearing, and shutdown to
  preserve profile isolation and prevent cleared data from reappearing.

Available for Minecraft 26.1.2 and 26.2 on Fabric.
